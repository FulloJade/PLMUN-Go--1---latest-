Admissions to PLMun

Welcome to the admissions page of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa. We strive to make higher education accessible and affordable for the citizens of Muntinlupa and beyond.

How to Apply

Follow this step-by-step roadmap to complete your application process.

1

Visit the Admission Portal

Start your application online via the official PLMun Admission Portal.

2

Submit Requirements

Depending on the program, submit your high school transcript, recommendation letters, and supporting documents.

3

Entrance Exam / Interview (if required)

Some programs may ask for entrance exams or interviews. Check specific course requirements.

4

Scholarship Application

If you need financial aid, apply for the PLMun Educational Assistance Program (Ordinance No. 18-183).

5

Enrollment

Once accepted, enroll through the student portal and finalize your schedule.

FREE TERTIARY EDUCATION

Tuition and Fees

PLMun is a beneficiary of the Free Higher Tertiary Education Program under UniFAST, which means eligible students may enjoy free tuition and reduced miscellaneous fees.

Admission Deadlines

Admission deadlines vary by semester. Please check the official PLMun portal regularly for updated cut-off dates and submission schedules.

Financial Aid Competitiveness

Financial aid is competitive. Ensure you complete all document requirements early to secure your slot for municipal assistance programs.

Admission Center Location

For questions or on-site inquiries, reach out to the Admission Center located in the Bonifacio Building (as listed in the campus office directory).
