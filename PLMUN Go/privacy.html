Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

PLMUN Go is a campus navigation website for Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa. It has no accounts and no login, and we do not track you.

Camera

The AR guide and QR scanning use your camera only while the page is open. The video is processed on your device. It is never recorded, uploaded or shared.

Saved on your device

Your browser may save the location you set, your step-free route choice, and a small list of your recent questions to the assistant. This stays in your own browser and is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Contact form

If you send us a message, the name, email and message you type are sent through Formspree to our team email. We use them only to reply to you.

Third-party content

Some content, such as the Google Maps view and the icon fonts, is loaded from other services. Like any website, they may receive standard request information such as your IP address.

Your rights

We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). To ask about, correct or delete a message you sent us, email PLMUNGo@gmail.com.
