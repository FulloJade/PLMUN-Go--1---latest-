ABOUT PLMUN

Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa

Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa (PLMun) is a premier public university committed to providing quality, affordable, and accessible education to the youth of Muntinlupa City. Since its establishment, PLMun has nurtured graduates who embody excellence, integrity, and service to the community.

Our Journey

Tracing our roots from local college to respected university

1991

Foundation

Founded by the local government of Muntinlupa to offer higher education opportunities for deserving students.

Expansion

Growth & Development

Evolved from a local college into a fully recognized university offering diverse degree programs.

Present

Academic Excellence

Nurturing thousands of students across multiple colleges and specialized fields of study.

Vision

A dynamic and highly competitive Higher Education Institution (HEI) committed to people empowerment towards building a humane society.

Mission

To provide quality, affordable and relevant education responsive to the changing needs of the local and global communities through effective and efficient integration of instruction, research and extension; to develop productive and God-loving individuals in society.
