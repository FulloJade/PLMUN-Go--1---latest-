PLMUN Go · Location QR Codes

Scan one of these and the website knows where you are standing, so directions start from there.

How to use: edit the list in the script below (the place must match a name in your University Office Directory, e.g. "University Library"). Print this page (Ctrl+P), cut the codes, and post each one at its spot (entrance, stairs, hallway). You can also type a place here and press Add.
